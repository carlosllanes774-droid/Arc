Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Arc (“we”, “our”) provides personalized meal planning and nutrition guidance through the Arc app at nutriai-qevt.onrender.com and related services.

Information we collect

  • Account: email address and authentication identifiers when you sign in with Google or email.
  • Profile: name, age, weight, height, sex, activity level, goals, dietary preferences, and meal plans you create.
  • Usage: meals logged through Coach, weekly reflections, and app preferences.
  • Location (optional): approximate area for regional grocery estimates — only if you allow it on your device.

How we use information

We use your information to generate meal plans, grocery lists, nutrition estimates, and Coach responses. To provide AI features, Arc may send non-identifying profile data (age, sex, weight, height, goals, preferences) to AI and nutrition API providers. We do not sell your personally identifiable information.

Third-party services

We use Supabase (authentication and storage), OpenAI, Spoonacular, Edamam, USDA, and optionally Kroger for recipes, nutrition data, and pricing. Each provider has its own privacy policy.

Data retention & deletion

You can delete your Arc account and cloud profile from Settings → Delete account. You may also contact us to request deletion.

Security

API keys are stored on our servers, not in the app. Access to your profile is protected by authentication and database security rules.

Children

Arc is not intended for children under 13.

Not medical advice

Arc provides general nutrition and meal-planning information. It is not medical advice. Consult a healthcare professional for medical or dietary conditions.

Contact

Questions: see Contact in Arc Settings or email the address listed there.
